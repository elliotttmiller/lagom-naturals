Privacy

We keep as little data as a store can and still deliver your files. This page lists what we hold, why, and how to have it removed.

Updated 26 September 2026
On this page
  1. 01What we collect
  2. 02Why we hold it
  3. 03Checkout and payment
  4. 04Email
  5. 05Cookies and analytics
  6. 06Your rights
01

What we collect

Your email address and name when you buy or subscribe, the order details our checkout provider sends us, and the messages you send through the contact page. Nothing else is stored on our side.

02

Why we hold it

To deliver the files, send the update emails for products you own, answer your messages and keep the records that tax law asks a store to keep.

03

Checkout and payment

Payments are handled by a third-party checkout provider. We never see or store card numbers. The provider has its own privacy policy that applies to the payment step.

04

Email

If you take the free guide or subscribe, you get the emails you asked for and can unsubscribe with one click in any of them. Update emails for products you own are sent as part of the purchase.

05

Cookies and analytics

The site uses privacy-friendly analytics that do not track you across other sites. The checkout provider may set the cookies it needs to complete a payment.

06

Your rights

Write to us at any time to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you. We answer within one working day and delete within seven, except records we are required to keep for tax purposes.

Questions about this page? Write to hello@shelfline.co
